caravan.

Clinic check-in companion

The passenger seat of care.

Caravan gives patients big, plain-spoken check-in screens that adjust to how they answer, and gives the front desk one view of who has arrived and who needs a hand.

The name: people travelling together, with help close by.

Body text is ink on blush (about 10:1 contrast). Hot pink is only used for large type or as a fill under white text, because pink text on blush is too faint to read.
