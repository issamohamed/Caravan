Family Medicine · phone check-in

Return visit check-in

Live on patients' phones · version 4 · published Oct 20 by MR · 5 questions

Preview on a phone

Wording follows the patient's answers, never their age. Patients can switch wording on any screen.

  1. 1

    Phone and address

    Yes / No
    Simple words

    Is this still your phone number and address?

    Medical terms

    Confirm the contact information on file (phone, address).

  2. 2

    Medicines: sets the wording

    Choice · branches
    Simple words

    Do you know the names of the medicines you take?

    Medical terms

    Medication reconciliation: can you list your current medications?

    What each answer does

    • Yes, I know them→medication list, medical terms
    • Some / Not really→pill-bottle question, simple words
    • I don't take any→skip to question 4
  3. 3

    Medicines: follow-up

    Picked by question 2
    Simple words

    Did you bring your pill bottles or a list?

    Medical terms

    Review your medication list. Mark each one Taking, Stopped or Changed.

  4. 4

    Reason for visit

    Pick one, or say it
    Simple words

    What's the main reason for your visit today?

    Medical terms

    Reason for visit (main concern).

  5. 5

    Insurance card

    Edited · not published
    Simple words

    Is this the same insurance card as last time?

    Medical terms

    Verify coverage: same payer and member ID as on file?

Readability check

  • Simple wording reading levelGrade 3–5
  • Longest simple question11 words
  • Works with Read aloudAll 5
  • Medical terms shownOnly when chosen

How updates reach phones

  1. Edit here, then publish.
  2. It's saved as a new version of the clinic's questionnaire (FHIR Questionnaire, v5).
  3. Phones load the new version at the next check-in. No app update needed.
